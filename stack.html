09 · Proposed technology stack

Azure-centred, open at the seams

One cloud keeps identity, networking, secrets and billing in one place, and gives a single security model (Entra ID) across people, services and agents. Every seam, including models, bus, workflow and observability, uses an open or standard interface so it can be replaced.

Model routing policy

Task classModel tierExamplesWhy
Classify / route / detectSmall, fastTicket triage, email intent, language detectionHigh volume, narrow output space; about 10-30× cheaper.
Extract to schemaSmall or mid + OCRSupplier confirmations, invoices, packing listsStructured outputs and a verifier catch errors; escalate to frontier on low confidence.
Converse with customersMidWISMO, care advice, returnsTone and grounding matter; latency matters.
Reason over trade-offsFrontierProcurement options, incident analysis, Buy PanelLow volume, high stakes.
Judge / verifyA different model family from the maker where possibleReply QA, extraction verification, AuditorReduces correlated errors between maker and checker.
