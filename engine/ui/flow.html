HOW TO EXPLAIN THIS AGENT

Explain the AI and ML in plain English

Rules calculate and constrain

Demand, order quantities, spending limits and approval tiers come from explicit code. The system can run these checks even when the language model is unavailable.

Ridge predicts ad orders

It learns a weighted relationship between advertising signals and future orders. A penalty keeps those weights from becoming extreme. Its prediction informs the advertising rules; it does not grant permission to spend.

Language models read and investigate

DistilBERT classifies review text. A separate tool-using model can investigate and choose a validated option. Invalid answers fall back to deterministic rules.

The ad-model training panel is simulated. A high test score on that panel is not evidence of production accuracy.
