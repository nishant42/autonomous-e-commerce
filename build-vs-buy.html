08 · Build vs buy

Buy the plumbing, build the brain and the brakes

The rule: buy where the problem is solved, regulated or commoditised, such as checkout, payments, statutory accounting, warehousing and EDI transport. Build where the company's advantage or safety lives: the decision logic, the agent runtime, the Action Gateway and the operational ledger that ties it all together.

What I would specifically not buy

An "AI agents for e-commerce" suite that owns decisions end to end. It would own our most important logic, hide the tool loop and the audit trail, and make our safety case depend on a vendor's prompt. We buy the model, not the judgement.
