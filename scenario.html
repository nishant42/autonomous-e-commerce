Timeline

T+0 · Tue 10:02

Detect: Demand Sensing

A CUSUM change-point detector on the order stream crosses threshold: 9.3/day vs 3.0 plan over 36h. It emits demand.anomaly and opens case CASE-2026-0930-017. The LLM classifies the likely cause from tool data: 71% of sessions come from one interior-design publication referral, and there is no campaign of ours running. Cause: organic, external.

T+1 min

Validate: Fraud Screening

First question: is the demand real? 311 distinct customers, normal card-country mix, Radar risk distribution within baseline, no address clustering. Verdict: genuine demand. If it had been a fraud ring, the flow would stop here: hold the orders and never place a PO.

T+3 min

Re-forecast: Forecasting Agent

An intraday re-forecast uses a spike-decay prior learned from past external-referral events: P50 9.1/day, tapering to 1.6× baseline over about 3 weeks. P90 is 13.4/day. The forecast is tagged anomaly_influenced=true, which automatically lifts the tier of any downstream PO.

T+4 min

Position: Inventory Position Service

ATP 42 at P50 gives 2.6 days of cover, so the critical band. Inbound 60 units arrive in 12 days, a 9-day gap. Node transfer EU→UK is evaluated and rejected: EU cover is also critical, and the cross-border landed cost exceeds the benefit. The rejection is logged with its reason.

T+5 min

Adjust demand: Marketing

The coupling rule for critical cover fires. The Budget Allocator pauses paid acquisition on the SKU (T0; the €140/day it was spending moves to sister SKUs with more than 10 weeks of cover). The Growth Strategist asks the Creative Agent for a "Complete the Étoile set" email to the waitlist of related SKUs. It passes the Brand Guardian and is published (T1). Back-in-stock capture is enabled on the product page.

T+5 min

Adjust price: Pricing Agent

Ending the 10% promotion is proposed and executed (T0). Price Guard passes it: within band, first change this week, and reference-price rules satisfied. A scarcity uplift is not proposed, because policy allows scarcity pricing only when no confirmed inbound exists within lead time, and 60 units are due in 12 days.

T+8 min

Plan: Replenishment Planner

Order-up-to over lead time P90 (33 days) with a 97% service level gives 240 units. MOQ and case pack are satisfied. Cover after receipt is 11 weeks, under the 16-week cap.

T+10 min

Decide: Procurement Agent + Buy Panel

The PO Builder prices 240 units at €6,720, which would normally be T1. Because the forecast is anomaly-influenced, policy lifts it to T2 → Buy Panel. Frozen decision pack; three personas in parallel:

  • Demand Planner: 240, since P90 demand over lead time justifies it.
  • CFO: 180, since spikes from external referrals decay and overstock ties up €1.7k of cash for about 5 months.
  • Risk Officer: supplier OTIF 94% and the price is stable, so approve either; prefers a split delivery.

Synthesis: majority → 180 units now, plus a scheduled review in 10 days for up to 60 more, and a request to expedite the existing 60. The Action Gateway executes CreatePurchaseOrder with key po:sup_yd:ETOILE-BATH-BLANC:2026-W40:v1.

T+12 min

Transmit: Supplier Liaison

For this supplier, POs go by EDI 850 through the EDI provider. The 997 functional acknowledgement arrives within minutes. The expedite request goes by email to the account manager, because the supplier has no API for expedites: a template with PO facts plus a short LLM-written courteous paragraph. Outbound goes only to the verified supplier domain.

T+26 h

Interpret the reply: maker-checker

The supplier replies: 120 units can ship in 5 days, 60 in 3 weeks, expedite fee €380, price unchanged. The Doc Extractor turns this into JSON. The Verifier checks the PO reference exists, quantities sum to 180, the price matches the contract price list and the fee sits within the expedite policy (≤ 8% of PO value). The result is an AmendPO for the split delivery at T1. The PO state moves to Acknowledged → Amended.

T+26 h

Finance

Commitment of €5,040 plus €380 is recorded. Open-to-buy for the supplier is reduced. The 13-week cash forecast is updated: runway 18.6 weeks, above the 12-week floor. Landed cost is recalculated with the fee (+€2.11 per unit spread across the 180) and sent to Pricing. Margin stays above the floor.

T+2 days

Stock-out and customer care

ATP reaches 0 at UK. Because inbound is confirmed within 10 days, the Order Orchestrator switches the SKU to backorder mode (T1) with a clear "ships by 9 Oct" message. The Care Resolution Agent answers delivery-date questions from the Ledger's PO dates, never from a guess. It proactively emails the 14 backordered customers. Any customer may cancel for a full refund (T0).

T+6 days

Fulfilment: Logistics and 3PL

The ASN (EDI 856) arrives. The Inbound Coordinator creates the 3PL inbound notice and requests priority receiving. The receipt count is 120, matching the ASN. ATP goes up, backorders are allocated first, then Shopify stock is pushed. The Lifecycle agent triggers back-in-stock emails to 212 waitlisted customers (T0, consent-checked).

T+7 days

Recover demand generation

Cover reaches 5.2 weeks, above the 5-week exit threshold for "tight" (hysteresis). The Budget Allocator resumes SKU acquisition ads at 50% and ramps up by bandit.

T+10 days

Close the loop

Scheduled review: velocity has decayed to 1.7× baseline, close to the CFO's view. The planner recommends 30 more units, not 60, as a T0 PO. The case closes. The Auditor reviews the case. The Forecasting Agent adds the event to its external-spike training set. The invoice later three-way matches, and payment is scheduled to terms. The owner's weekly report says: "Organic spike on Étoile towel handled; 14 backorders, 0 cancellations, +€4.9k gross margin vs baseline."

What would have made a human get involved?
  • A PO above €60k (T3), or the panel returning a split verdict.
  • The supplier asking to change bank details or requesting prepayment (always T3).
  • Fraud Screening finding that the spike was a coordinated attack. The Control Tower would freeze the SKU and page the owner.
  • The supplier rejecting the expedite and no other supplier SKU being available, with backorders exceeding 30 days. The Incident Commander would brief the owner on customer-communication options.
