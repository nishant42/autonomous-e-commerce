02

Censored-demand correction

Feature pipeline

Zero sales on a stock-out day doesn't mean zero demand. Masking those days stops the model from learning "nobody wanted it".

Days with < 90% in-stock hours are masked from the level and seasonality estimates (masked loss), not treated as zero.
03

Segmentation · ADI / CV²

ML ops

Each series is classified by how often it sells (ADI) and how variable the sizes are (CV²). The segment picks the model family.

Syntetos-Boylan thresholds: ADI 1.32, CV² 0.49. Products with under 8 weeks of history go to cold start.
04

Train the model portfolio

Azure ML · nightly

Every product gets baselines plus the model family for its segment. Winners are highlighted after the backtest.

In-browser stand-ins: the "LightGBM" model here is a feature-based quantile learner with the same inputs and outputs (level, day-of-week, lag-364 seasonality, empirical quantiles); production uses LightGBM with quantile loss. Croston-SBA, TSB, ETS and seasonal naive are implemented as specified.
05

Rolling-origin backtest and promotion gate

Gate

Three origins × 28-day horizons on held-out data. The winner must beat the seasonal-naive baseline, or the baseline is published instead.

Winner = lowest pinball loss within the segment's model family; it must also beat seasonal-naive. WAPE = Σ|y − ŷ| / Σy · bias = (Σŷ − Σy) / Σy · pinball averaged over P10/P50/P90 · coverage = share of actuals inside P10-P90.
06

Hierarchical reconciliation

MinT (OLS here)

The collection total is forecast separately. Children and parent are reconciled so the numbers add up everywhere.

ỹ = S (SᵀS)⁻¹ Sᵀ ŷ for the Étoile collection (parent + 3 children), 28-day totals. Production uses MinT with a shrinkage covariance.
07

Conformal calibration

Calibration

The P10-P90 band should contain about 80% of outcomes. The width is scaled on backtest residuals until it does.

Split-conformal on backtest windows: find the smallest scale k so that the interval P50 ± k·(spread) covers ≥ 80%.

09

Purchase decision · Monte Carlo order-up-to

Planner → Procurement

5,000 simulated futures of demand over (lead time + review period). The order-up-to level S is the service-level quantile. Policy then sets the approval tier.

Lead time ~ lognormal(P50 24 d, P90 slider). Daily demand ~ normal fitted to the forecast P50/P90, truncated at 0. Review period 7 days. Qty = S − (on hand + on order 60), rounded up to case pack 6, capped at 16 weeks of cover. Unit cost €28 for the towel. Tiers: T0 ≤ €5k · T1 ≤ €20k · T2 ≤ €60k · T3 above; anomaly-influenced → at least T2.

+

Newsvendor · seasonal one-shot buy

Planner

For a new-season duvet cover, unsold stock goes to the outlet. The best buy is a quantile of season demand.

Cu = price − cost, Co = cost − outlet value, critical ratio = Cu / (Cu + Co), Q* = F⁻¹(CR). Season demand ~ Normal(400, 120) from the analog forecast. Expected profit estimated from 4,000 simulated seasons.
